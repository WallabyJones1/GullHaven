**Short description (the line under the title):**

A cosy fishing game of tides, islands and one old mystery.

---

**Description:**

Fifty years ago, the lighthouse at Gullhaven went dark. Its great lens, the Gull's Eye,
vanished one stormy night, and so did the keeper.

Now Gran has left you her hut on the point and her old rod. Old Marlo on the docks will
show you the ropes. The rest is up to you.

**A whole coast to fish.** Harbour, sandy shallows, mangroves, seagrass, coral reef, kelp
forest, a wreck graveyard, a turquoise lagoon, open blue, the deep, and a clear mountain
lake. What bites depends on where you cast, the time of day, the tide, the weather, the
moon and the season.

**More than 300 things to catch and find.** Fish, legends, crabs and lobsters, shells, sea
glass, fossils, treasure, letters in bottles, and rare golden, albino, moonlit and rainbow
variants.

**Fish that fight back.** Runners, jumpers, divers and darters, each with its own fight.
Tough fish go on the reel bar. Fisher levels and skills, better rods, and new ways to fish
as you go: crab pots, cast nets, trolling, and a spearfishing dive.

**A story to follow.** Follow the keeper's marks across six islands, through puzzles,
riddles and a pike that never takes a hook twice.

**Make it yours.** Make your fisher, dress them up at Threads & Tides, buy boats and paint
them, upgrade Gran's hut and fill it with furniture, and tame a harbour cat to follow you
about.

**A coast that feels alive.** HD-2D pixel art in a lit 3D world, with weather, a real
day-night cycle, gulls, dolphins, whales, feeding frenzies, and townsfolk with things to
say.

**Beta notes:**

This is an early beta. Expect rough edges, and please tell us about them. Your save is
kept on your computer between versions.

- Windows: unzip and run Gullhaven.exe. If Windows SmartScreen appears, click More info,
  then Run anyway (the game isn't code-signed yet).
- macOS: unzip and open Gullhaven.app. The first time, go to System Settings > Privacy &
  Security and click Open Anyway.
- Linux: unzip and run Gullhaven.x86_64.

Plays best with a controller or keyboard. Every prompt shows the right button.
